The Negarit Architecture

How Negarit Operates in 4 Steps

From raw environmental telemetry to verified citizen action, every step is built to save lives while preventing false alarm panic.

01

AI Predicts 4 Disasters

Analyzes real-time Open-Meteo 16-day climate models and USGS seismic feeds to calculate hazard risk scores for Drought, Flood, Landslides, and Earthquakes before they unfold.

16-Day Forecast & Rift Geophysics
02

Federal SMS Approval

Early warning cell broadcasts cannot be sent automatically. Federal government officials review drafted messages, inspect populations at risk, and must authorize before public release.

Strict Verification Guardrail
03

Verify With Community

Agencies are NOT notified just for hypothetical forecasts. When disaster strikes and reports come in, we cross-check weather data and verify with our local volunteer members on the ground.

Ground-Truth Volunteer Mesh
04

Deploy Aid & Hospitality

Once verified, we grant emergency agencies access to our GIS maps and weather telemetry, connecting them directly with local volunteer members to coordinate hospitality, food, and shelter.

Seamless Agency-Community Aid
Predictive Engine

The 4 Natural Hazards Predicted by Negarit

Telemetry from Open-Meteo climate sensors and USGS seismic streams continuously feed these 4 models.

🌾 PASTORAL ARIDITY

Drought & Water Stress

Monitors topsoil moisture (0-7cm) < 0.16 m³/m³ and 16-day rainfall deficits across Afar, Somali, and Borena pastoral regions.

Trigger: Soil < 0.16 m³/m³
Response: Water trucking & forage
🌊 RIVER BASIN SURGE

Flash Floods & Inundation

Flags 24h rainfall > 25mm combined with saturated soil along Awash River, Baro Catchment, and Lake Tana tributaries.

Trigger: Rain > 25mm / 24h
Response: Basin evacuation & shelter
⛰️ SLOPE INSTABILITY

Highland Landslides

Calculates Highland Shear Stress (Daily Rain × 1.5 + Soil Saturation × 50) on steep mountain slopes in Gofa, North Shewa, and Simien.

Trigger: Rain > 85mm & Saturation
Response: Hillside evacuation & SAR
🌋 TECTONIC RIFT

Seismic Rift Activity

Streams USGS real-time seismic sensors along the Main Ethiopian Rift, tracking tremors and active fault shifts from Hawassa to Afar.

Trigger: Magnitude ≥ M4.5
Response: Structural inspection & safety
